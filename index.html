My First Survival World

October 3, 2026 by Ti-jean Dehaney

Starting a brand new survival world is always exciting. The first night is the most important, so I spent the first few minutes punching trees, gathering wood, and crafting a basic set of tools. By sunset I had a small wooden shelter on top of a hill, which gave me a good view of the land around me.

Over the next few days I expanded the shelter into a proper base with a farm, a storage room, and a small animal pen. Planning the layout felt a lot like solving a puzzle, deciding where everything should go so that it was easy to reach. It reminded me of how much I enjoy organising things step by step, whether it is a sudoku grid or a webpage.

Into the Caves

October 10, 2026 by Ti-jean Dehaney

This week I finally gathered enough courage to explore the caves under my base. I packed plenty of torches, some food, and a stone pickaxe, and placed torches along the way so I could always find my path back. The deeper I went, the more ores I found, including iron, coal, and even a few rare gems.

Caving is all about being careful and patient. Every corner could hide a new tunnel or a surprise, so I learned to light up each area before moving on. Coming back to the surface with a full inventory was one of the most satisfying moments in the game so far, and I am already planning my next trip underground.
